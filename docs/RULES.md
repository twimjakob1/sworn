# The Rules of Sworn

These are the rules the tested model enforces. Every number is a proposal until launch.

1. The supply is 1,000,000,000 coins, created once.
2. No one can create coins, freeze an account, reverse a transfer or change a balance. This includes the founder.
3. Checks that involve the founder or the founder’s company add nothing to the founder’s usage milestone.
4. Coin owners with 20% of the coins outside the founder’s circle can block a founder release during its notice period.
5. A checker posts at least 10,000 coins before verifying anything.
6. The party who posts a claim pays a fee in coins, 20 at the least. 80% pays the checker whose finding stands, and 20% goes to the community pool.
7. When a challenge overturns a finding, the checker’s share of the fee pays the challenger.
8. A claim that no checker takes up within 30 days can be withdrawn. The fee and the bond return in full.
9. A check reads the real system. The operator’s own statement carries no weight.
10. Work reported finished that the system shows unfinished sends the operator’s dollar bond to the customer.
11. A false finding costs the checker 50% of its stake, split between the challenger and the community pool.
12. A finding can be challenged for 7 days. A failed challenge costs the challenger 100 coins.
13. A finding is judged against the system as it stood on the day the finding was made.
14. An issuer can post a sworn statement about its own coin for 30 to 365 days, with a bond of at least 10,000 coins behind it. A statement that reads true at its first check is in force for the term.
15. Anyone can report a statement in force. A statement that reads false forfeits its bond: 20% to the reporter and 80% to the community pool. A report against a statement that still reads true costs the reporter 100 coins, paid to the issuer.
16. A statement that stands through its term is recorded as honored, and its bond returns to the issuer.
17. Every finished check is entered in a ledger where each receipt carries the fingerprint of the one before it.

Rule 10 describes a bond in dollars. That bond is in the model and is left out of the first launch, where every bond is in coins.

## Who Receives the Coins

| Share | Coins | Part |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free distribution to users | 500,000,000 | 50% |
| Community pool | 400,000,000 | 40% |
| Founder vault | 100,000,000 | 10% |

The project sells no coins. The rule for the free distribution has not been written.
